## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Our proposed solution is FarmDirect, a digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, hotels, and bulk buyers.
Farmers can register and upload their agricultural products with quantity and expected prices.
Consumers can directly purchase fresh products without depending on multiple middlemen.
The platform provides transparent pricing so farmers receive better profits and consumers get affordable products.
AI predicts market demand and suggests suitable prices to reduce wastage.
A logistics system connects farmers with transport services for timely delivery.
Online payment and order tracking make the buying and selling process easier.
Innovation and Uniqueness:
Direct farmer-to-consumer connection.
AI-based demand forecasting.
Smart transportation and route optimization.
Transparent pricing and secure payments.

## Technical Approach
AI/ML: Python, Pandas and Scikit-learn.
Maps and Location: Google Maps API.
Cloud: Firebase or AWS.
Implementation Methodology:
Develop a user-friendly website for farmers and consumers.
Create separate login accounts for farmers, buyers and delivery partners.
Store product, customer, order and payment details in the database.
Integrate AI to predict product demand based on previous sales and market trends.
Use route optimization to identify efficient delivery routes.
Test the platform with sample farmers, products and customer orders.
Working Process:
Farmer Registration → Product Upload → Customer Order → Payment → Logistics Assignment → Product Delivery.

## Feasibility and Viability
The system can initially be implemented in selected villages and expanded to other regions.
Existing transportation services can be used for product delivery.
Potential Challenges:
Some farmers may have limited technical knowledge.
Transportation and storage facilities may be unavailable in certain areas.
Product prices and demand may change frequently.
Internet connectivity may be limited in rural areas.
Solutions to Overcome Challenges:
Provide a simple interface with regional language support.
Partner with local transport providers and warehouses.
Update prices and demand information regularly.
Provide an easy-to-use mobile-friendly platform with low-data usage.

## Impact and Benefits
